QUESTIONS

The Ones I Get Asked Every Week

I've never written copy for a client. Is the Igniter right for me?

Yes. Most of my students haven't. Lauren was a receptionist. Yolanda was a teacher. Trey had never posted a single copywriting reel. The Igniter starts at fundamentals and walks you through the first client pitch by week 4.

What's the difference between the Igniter and the 1:1 mentorship?

The Igniter is the group program and the right first step for 90% of people. The 1:1 is for women already working as copywriters who want deeper, private support to triple their rates. You can do the Igniter first and graduate into the 1:1 later. Most of my mentees do.

How long until I book a client?

Execution dependent. The students who follow the pitch cadence typically land a first client in weeks 4-8. The students who keep "learning one more thing" before sending a pitch take longer. Everything we teach only works if you send the messages.

Do I have to write for course creators specifically?

No, but I recommend it. It's the niche I know inside out and it pays the best by a wide margin. If you already have a niche you love, the frameworks carry over. I'll help you adapt them.

Who is this NOT for?

Women looking for a hobby. Women who want to consume another course and never pitch. Women who want a male money-math coach yelling at them about discipline. If any of that is the vibe, we're not a fit and that's okay.
